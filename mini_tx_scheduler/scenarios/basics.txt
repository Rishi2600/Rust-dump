# Six transactions arrive before the first block. Between them they show
# every ordering rule: priority by fee rate, a sender's own order, clashes
# over a shared account, a read that waits for a write, and a failure.

account alice 1000
account bob   1000
account carol 1000
account dave    50
account erin  1000

slot 0
tx alice pays shop 100 fee 10                        # 0.10 per unit
tx bob   pays shop 200 fee 80                        # 0.80: also pays the shop, and goes first
tx carol pays frank 50 fee 40                        # touches nobody else's accounts
tx alice pays grace  5 fee 500                       # pays the most, but is alice's second
tx dave  pays shop 900 fee 20                        # dave has 50: it fails, and still pays its fee
tx erin  pays heidi 10 fee 30 units 400 reads shop   # only reads the shop
